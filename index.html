About Me

Dedicated to software engineering principles, backend systems, and crafting responsive user experiences.

I’m a Software Engineering student at Ahram Canadian University, Faculty of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, with a strong interest in Software Engineering, Web Development, Backend Development, and System Design.

I enjoy transforming real-world problems and business requirements into practical, reliable, and user-focused software solutions. My approach focuses on building applications with clean architecture, well-structured databases, maintainable code, and intuitive user experiences.
